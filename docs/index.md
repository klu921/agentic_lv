# Long-Context Video Understanding Research

Welcome to our research blog on long-context video understanding. We explore how to leverage Large Language Models (LLMs) and Visual Language Models (VLMs) to understand and reason about long-form video content.

## Research Focus

Our work addresses the fundamental challenge of understanding multi-hour videos - a problem that extends far beyond the context windows of current AI systems. We propose an agentic pipeline that combines:

- **Visual Language Models** for frame-level understanding
- **Large Language Models** for temporal reasoning
- **Novel critic modules** for improved accuracy and robustness

## Key Achievements

- **65.18% accuracy** on LV-Bench dataset using only open-source models
- **5% improvement** from our novel critic module
- Efficient processing of multi-hour videos without end-to-end training
